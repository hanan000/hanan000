## 🧠 About me

AI engineer building **real-time voice agents and conversational AI** — from speech-to-text and LLM reasoning to text-to-speech, deployed as containerised services.

- 🎙️ **Voice agents** — real-time call-center and assistant agents on LiveKit (STT, TTS, turn detection, noise cancellation, tool calling)
- 💬 **Chatbots & RAG** — multi-platform chatbots (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Twilio) with retrieval over company knowledge bases
- 🔤 **NLP** — intent classification, question answering and BERT fine-tuning (multilingual and Turkish)
- 🛒 **Data & scraping** — product and news scrapers feeding search and shopping assistants
- ⚙️ **Backend & delivery** — FastAPI / Django services, Docker, CI/CD pipelines, Google Cloud
